# Screening rules v1.2 (locked October 29, 2026; amended twice the same day)

Applies to: the 183 meta-analyses in the evidence ledger, then the 257 systematic reviews. Screening is on title and abstract only.

## Include when R1, R2 and R3 are all met

R1. Condition. The review covers people with, or measures of, at least one tracked condition: ADHD; autism; depression; anxiety (including social anxiety); PTSD and trauma (including trauma exposure such as childhood maltreatment and adverse childhood experiences); OCD; bipolar disorder; eating disorders; body dysmorphia and body image disturbance; substance use; gambling problems; intellectual disability (including Down syndrome, fragile X, Williams syndrome); learning disabilities; brain injury; dementia and cognitive decline; psychosis and schizophrenia (including paranoia); suicidality and self-harm; blind and low vision; Deaf and hard of hearing; physical disability; neurological conditions (epilepsy, Parkinson's, MS, Huntington's, ALS, cerebral palsy, Tourette syndrome and tics, functional neurological disorder); speech, language and communication conditions (including AAC users); chronic illness and pain (including ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, long COVID); disability or mental illness in general. Symptom-level measures count (for example depressive symptoms, psychological distress, loneliness does NOT count on its own; wellbeing or life satisfaction alone does NOT count). A tracked condition analysed only as a subgroup or moderator counts.

R2. Online exposure. The review covers at least one tracked online exposure as people meet it in ordinary use: problematic or addictive use (internet, social media, smartphone, gaming, short video); cyberbullying and online harassment; harmful content (self-harm, eating disorder, graphic content); social comparison and appearance content; algorithmic recommendation; scams and fraud; online gambling and loot boxes; online buying and debt; dark patterns and consent; AI chatbots and companions; online sexual exploitation, grooming, sextortion, image-based abuse; misinformation; night-time use and sleep; attention capture and notifications; accessibility barriers; data, privacy and breaches; surveillance and monitoring; online peer support (peer support communities and digital peer support), with who ran it recorded at extraction: peers only and unmoderated, trained or moderated peers, clinician-led or clinician-supervised, or unclear. Online health information seeking counts when studied as a route to harm (cyberchondria, health anxiety, misinformation). General use or time on social media, internet, smartphones or games counts. Reviews mixing online and offline forms count when the online form is part of what was pooled or synthesized. "Screen time" counts only when it includes internet-connected use (television-only does not).

R3. Relation. The review reports a pooled or synthesized finding that links the condition and the online exposure, in either direction: the condition associated with or predicting the exposure; the exposure associated with or predicting the condition; a comparison of people with and without the condition on the exposure; or a pooled prevalence of the exposure or harm among people with the condition.

## Applied throughout

R4. The direction of the result is never a criterion. Null, protective, mixed and harmful findings screen in alike.
R5. Preprints are screened like any other record and keep their preprint flag.
R6. When the abstract leaves R1, R2 or R3 in doubt, the decision is UNCLEAR, which goes to full text and counts as included at this stage.

## Full text check

Records marked UNCLEAR, or where the two screeners disagree, go to a full text check under the same rules. A mixed online and offline review passes R2 only if the online form is part of what was pooled or synthesized; an online form mentioned only in the introduction or discussion does not count. Where the full text cannot be obtained, the decision is made on the title and abstract and the record is marked "full text not accessible".

## Exclusion codes (give the first that applies)

E1. No tracked condition (or only wellbeing, loneliness, self-esteem, personality traits, academic outcomes).
E2. No tracked online exposure (offline only, or television only).
E3. Condition and exposure both present but no finding linking them (for example, prevalence of problematic use in a general population with no condition analysis, or a scale validation).
E4. Clinical treatment delivered online and evaluated for efficacy (internet CBT, therapy apps, digital interventions, therapy chatbots tested as treatment). Online peer support and general-purpose or companion chatbots met in ordinary use are not E4.
E5. Not a quantitative synthesis (no pooling). Not excluded from the project: moved to the systematic review screen.
